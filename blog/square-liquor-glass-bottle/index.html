What This Equipment Does

A square liquor glass bottle earns a place on the glass bottle buyers production line by removing variation rather than by adding capacity. This build holds 18740–23240 units per hour on 5 L product, which is what makes downstream planning possible.

Key Technical Specifications

The three numbers that decide glass forming quality are output per hour, weight deviation and the mould section spacing. On this square liquor glass bottle the tolerance is ±1.5 % and the range is 5 L.

Integration With Your Line

A square liquor glass bottle hands over containers that a filler, capper or labeller will accept. Matching the glass bottle buyers line's downstream handling saves an inspection step that otherwise happens twice.

Capacity Planning and ROI

Model the square liquor glass bottle on total cost over the equipment life, not on purchase price. A unit with SUS304 with sanitary fittings contact parts and a 1.5 ml tolerance costs more to buy and less to run than a cheaper unit on the same glass bottle buyers production line.

Maintenance and Service

The parts worth stocking for a square liquor glass bottle are small, common and cheap — seals, guide rails, sensor sets. Holding them turns a glass bottle buyers shutdown into a shift adjustment instead of a week of waiting.

Frequently Asked Questions

What bottle sizes can this line cover?

The reference configuration covers the 5 L format range; other sizes need a tooling change.

What maintenance does it need?

Routine checks on the wear items and a scheduled service at the recommended interval.

How is the neck finish verified?

With a neck ring gauge and a go/no-go plug on the finished bottle.

How do I size this for my plant?

Does it integrate with existing lines?

Yes, with standard PLC interfaces and shared CIP or control logic when specified at quotation.
