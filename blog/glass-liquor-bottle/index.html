Glass Liquor Bottle

Published 2026-10-22 · Certified Glass Bottles

Specifying a glass liquor bottle for glass bottle buyers is an exercise in matching three things: the rate you need, the product you run and the conditions you run it in. This guide covers each of the three, and ends with the checks to complete before you sign off.

What This Equipment Does

A glass liquor bottle earns a place on the glass bottle buyers production line by removing variation rather than by adding capacity. This build holds 9837–13337 units per hour on 500 ml can product, which is what makes downstream planning possible.

Key Technical Specifications

Read a glass liquor bottle specification for what is measurable: rate 9837–13337 units per hour, deviation ±0.5 ml, container 500 ml can, control 4-inch HMI, load 6.5–8.5 kW, footprint 2400 × 1400 × 2400 mm, changeover 15 minutes. This build is quoted that way.

Integration With Your Line

A glass liquor bottle that runs beside the line rather than inside it will always be the bottleneck. This build accepts a standard encoder interface and a 2400 mm working width, which is what lets the glass bottle buyers production line treat it as one controlled step.

Capacity Planning and ROI

Compare the glass liquor bottle against the cost of the manual step it removes, including the labour during changeover. At 15 minutes per format change and 9837 units per hour, the saving is measurable in shift hours.

Maintenance and Service

Glass lines drift slowly rather than fail suddenly. Keeping the 4-inch HMI calibration and the timing log for the glass liquor bottle is what turns a quality complaint into a diagnosable fault.

Frequently Asked Questions

How is the neck finish verified?

With a neck ring gauge and a go/no-go plug on the finished bottle.

What maintenance does it need?

Routine checks on the wear items and a scheduled service at the recommended interval.

What bottle sizes can this line cover?

The reference configuration covers the 500 ml can format range; other sizes need a tooling change.

How do I size this for my plant?

Base capacity on peak hourly demand plus 15–20% headroom, and confirm utility availability before ordering.

Does it integrate with existing lines?

Yes, with standard PLC interfaces and shared CIP or control logic when specified at quotation.

What is typical lead time and service?

Standard machines ship in weeks; remote commissioning and spare parts support are usually included.
