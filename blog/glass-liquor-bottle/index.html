Specifying a glass liquor bottle for glass bottle buyers is an exercise in matching three things: the rate you need, the product you run and the conditions you run it in. This guide covers each of the three, and ends with the checks to complete before you sign off.

What This Equipment Does

A glass bottle buyers production line gains nothing from a glass liquor bottle that performs well on paper. This build is specified at 9837–13337 units per hour for 500 ml can product with the deviation defined, so the performance can be measured rather than assumed.

Key Technical Specifications

The three figures that move the price most are accuracy, contact-part grade and the control platform. On this glass liquor bottle the tolerance is 0.5 ml, the contact parts are SUS304 with sanitary fittings, and the controls run on a 4-inch HMI.

Integration With Your Line

A glass liquor bottle that runs beside the line rather than inside it will always be the bottleneck. This build accepts a standard encoder interface and a 2400 mm working width, which is what lets the glass bottle buyers production line treat it as one controlled step.

Capacity Planning and ROI

Capacity is only worth what you can sell. A glass liquor bottle at 9837–13337 units per hour should be sized against contracted demand for 500 ml can product, otherwise the ROI depends on a market the glass bottle buyers operation does not control.

Maintenance and Service

For a glass bottle buyers operation the maintenance question for a glass liquor bottle is simple: can the wear parts be changed by your own team during a scheduled break rather than by a technician on site?
