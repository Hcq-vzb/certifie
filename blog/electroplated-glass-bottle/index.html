An electroplated glass bottle for glass bottle buyers is a long-term commitment, and the specification is where that commitment is either made explicit or left ambiguous. This guide makes it explicit.

What This Equipment Does

The practical test of an electroplated glass bottle is not its quoted maximum but its behaviour at 18368 units per hour when product density shifts and viscosity climbs — the situation the glass bottle buyers production line meets every day.

Key Technical Specifications

For a glass bottle buyers buyer, the specification worth writing into the order is: SUS304 with sanitary fittings product-contact parts, 7-inch colour touch controls, 3.0 ml tolerance, 8-minute changeover, 7.5–10.5 kW connected load and a 1200 × 1900 × 3200 mm footprint.

Integration With Your Line

Buffer sizing upstream of the electroplated glass bottle determines whether the line absorbs a changeover. With 18368–22868 units per hour and a 8-minute format change, the buffer has to cover the stop, not the run.

Capacity Planning and ROI

Model the electroplated glass bottle on a difficult month rather than an average one. A line running 2 L product with a 8-minute changeover either holds up or it does not, and the investment should be judged on the harder case.

Maintenance and Service

The electroplated glass bottle was specified with wear part lead times in mind, so a glass bottle buyers operation is not left waiting for a section while the line stands at 18368 units per hour.

Frequently Asked Questions

How is the neck finish verified?

With a neck ring gauge and a go/no-go plug on the finished bottle.

What maintenance does it need?

Routine checks on the wear items and a scheduled service at the recommended interval.

What bottle sizes can this line cover?

The reference configuration covers the 2 L format range; other sizes need a tooling change.

How do I size this for my plant?

Base capacity on peak hourly demand plus 15–20% headroom, and confirm utility availability before ordering.

Does it integrate with existing lines?

Yes, with standard PLC interfaces and shared CIP or control logic when specified at quotation.

What is typical lead time and service?

Standard machines ship in weeks; remote commissioning and spare parts support are usually included.
