What This Equipment Does

A glass bottle mould design earns its place by holding its rate through a full campaign. This configuration is specified at 2251–3251 units per hour for 330 ml product, with the tolerance defined rather than implied.

Key Technical Specifications

Compare rated output, weight tolerance and the forming section count, not only the machine price. This glass bottle mould design is specified at 2251–3251 units per hour with a 10-inch colour touch and SUS304 with sanitary fittings contact parts.

Integration With Your Line

Agree the interface list before the order: encoder, safety relay, product detection and CIP return. This glass bottle mould design already handles a standard encoder interface and a 3200 mm working width.

Capacity Planning and ROI

Run the ROI on the worst month, not the average one. A glass bottle mould design rated at 2251–3251 units per hour will look different in peak season than in a quiet month, and the investment case has to survive both.

Maintenance and Service

Treat the glass bottle mould design service records as production data. Once a few months of interventions are logged, the failure pattern on the seals and the drive usually tells you where the real risk sits.

Frequently Asked Questions

How is the neck finish verified?

With a neck ring gauge and a go/no-go plug on the finished bottle.

What maintenance does it need?

Routine checks on the wear items and a scheduled service at the recommended interval.

What bottle sizes can this line cover?

The reference configuration covers the 330 ml format range; other sizes need a tooling change.

How do I size this for my plant?

Base capacity on peak hourly demand plus 15–20% headroom, and confirm utility availability before ordering.

Does it integrate with existing lines?

Yes, with standard PLC interfaces and shared CIP or control logic when specified at quotation.

What is typical lead time and service?

Standard machines ship in weeks; remote commissioning and spare parts support are usually included.
