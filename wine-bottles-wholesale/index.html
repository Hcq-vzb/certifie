Wine Bottles Wholesale: Buyer's Guide

Wholesale wine bottle buying is decided by five things: the bottle shape your wine style expects, the glass color, the closure it accepts, the customization you need, and how the bottles are packed for shipping.

Glass color and light protection

Antique green and amber glass protect light-sensitive wines; flint (clear) glass is chosen for visual presentation, usually for whites and rosés consumed young. Color also affects how label and capsule colors read on the shelf.

Closure compatibility

Confirm the bore and finish before ordering closures: cork bore, screw-cap finish (STELVIN), and glass stopper tops are different bottle specifications. Matching capsules and wirehoods for sparkling presentations must be ordered to the same neck dimension.

Customization options

  • Frosting, acid etching and enamel spraying
  • Decal (ceramic label) and screen printing
  • Embossing of logos into the glass mold
  • Matching capsules, wirehoods and stoppers

Wholesale buying points

  • Pallet packing: bottles ship in divider cartons on pallets; confirm carton counts and pallet size for your freight.
  • Breakage allowance: agree the inspection standard on arrival before shipment.
  • Lead time: custom colors and decoration add production time over stock shapes.
  • Mixed orders: combine shapes and closures in one shipment where the factory allows.

Frequently asked questions

Which bottle shape should I choose for my wine?

Follow the wine style convention: Bordeaux shapes for Cabernet and Merlot, Burgundy shapes for Pinot Noir and Chardonnay, tall flutes for Riesling styles. Convention helps buyers identify the wine style at a glance.

Does glass color matter for wine?

Yes. Antique green and amber glass filter UV light that degrades wine; clear flint glass shows the product but offers little light protection, suiting wines consumed young.

Can I order custom colors and decoration?

Yes — frosting, decals, screen printing and embossing are standard factory options. Custom colors and decoration add production lead time over stock shapes.

How are wine bottles packed for wholesale shipping?

In divider cartons on pallets to prevent contact between bottles. Agree carton counts, pallet dimensions and the arrival inspection standard before shipment.
